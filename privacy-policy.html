Privacy Policy for Snowball Rush

Effective date: October 17, 2026

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This privacy policy covers the Snowball Rush mobile game for Android (the "Game"), published on Google Play by OtiumGames ("we", "us", "our").

Summary

Snowball Rush does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no in-app purchases, and it works fully offline.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information or usage data. The Game:

  • does not ask you to create an account or sign in;
  • does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information;
  • does not contain advertising or advertising SDKs;
  • does not contain analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools;
  • does not use the advertising ID or any other device identifier;
  • does not connect to the internet, and requests no Android permissions at all (not even internet access).

Game progress saved on your device

The Game keeps a small save file in its private storage on your device so that you don't lose your progress. The file contains:

  • coins earned;
  • levels unlocked and completed, and stars earned;
  • shop items you have unlocked with in-game coins (upgrades and skins) and your equipped skin;
  • the route you took on your best run of each level (shown as a marker in the game);
  • simple gameplay totals, for example how many power-ups you have collected or penguins you have rescued;
  • your sound on/off setting.

This file never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its app data deletes it.

Sharing of information

Because we do not collect any information, we do not sell, rent, trade or share any personal information with third parties.

In-game coins and purchases

The Game has no in-app purchases and no real-money payments. Coins are earned only by playing and are spent only in the Game's shop. They have no real-world value and cannot be bought, sold or exchanged.

Children's privacy

The Game is suitable for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). The Game collects no data at all. It contains no ads, no chat, no social features and no external links that would let a child share personal information. If you believe a child has somehow sent us personal information, for example by email, please contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Third-party services

The Game does not include any third-party services or SDKs that collect information. You download the Game through Google Play, and Google's own privacy policy covers your use of the store.

Contacting us by email

If you choose to email us, we will receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to reply to you, and we delete it on request.

Security

No personal data is collected or sent anywhere, so we hold none that could be exposed. Your saved progress stays on your device, protected by your device's own security.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you through the Game, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can remove all locally saved game progress at any time by clearing the Game's data or uninstalling it.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the Game changes how it handles information, for example by adding online features, we will update this policy and change the dates above before that version is released.

Privacy Policy for Bottomless Miner: Idle Mining

Effective date: 24 October 2026

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This privacy policy covers the Bottomless Miner: Idle Mining mobile game for Android (the "Game", package com.otiumgames.bottomlessminer), published on Google Play by OtiumGames ("we", "us", "our"). Our other games have their own privacy policies.

Summary

Bottomless Miner does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no in-app purchases. Your progress is saved only on your device. It works fully offline and requests no Android permissions.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information or usage data. The Game:

  • does not ask you to create an account or sign in;
  • does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information;
  • does not contain advertising or advertising SDKs;
  • does not contain analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools;
  • does not use the advertising ID or any other device identifier;
  • does not send notifications;
  • does not connect to the internet, and requests no Android permissions at all (not even internet access).

Game progress saved on your device

The Game keeps a small save file in its private storage on your device (plus a backup copy, so a crash can't wipe your progress). It contains:

  • your coins, and the totals you have earned and spent;
  • how deep you have dug, which tiles you have dug, and drilling progress;
  • your crew (the in-game miner names, their upgrade levels, orders and current haul) and your Storage;
  • the in-game market's current price levels, and the ores you have found;
  • the date and time, taken from your device's clock, when you last played. The Game uses this to work out how much your crew dug while you were away (the "night shift");
  • your music on/off setting (in a separate small settings file).

The Game also keeps a short technical log of its most recent start-up on your device, to help it recover from a failed start. It contains no personal information.

These files never leave your device and are never sent to us or anyone else. They are not included in Android device backups. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its app data deletes them, and your progress cannot be recovered afterwards.

Progress while you are away is calculated on your device when you next open the Game. Nothing runs in the background while the Game is closed.

Sharing of information

Because we do not collect any information, we do not sell, rent, trade or share any personal information with third parties.

In-game coins and purchases

The Game has no in-app purchases and no real-money payments. Coins are earned only by playing and are spent only in the Game. They have no real-world value and cannot be bought, sold, withdrawn or exchanged. The in-game market and its ore prices are part of the game only; the Game is not a trading, investment or financial service.

Children's privacy

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). The Game collects no personal data at all, and has no ads, chat, social features or external links that would let a child share personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, please contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Third-party services

The Game does not include any third-party services or SDKs that collect information. You download the Game through Google Play, and Google's own privacy policy covers your use of the store.

Contacting us by email

If you choose to email us, we will receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to reply to you, and we delete it on request.

Security

We do not collect personal data, so we hold none that could be exposed. Your saved progress stays on your device, protected by your device's own security.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you through the Game, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can delete all locally saved progress at any time by clearing the Game's data or uninstalling it.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the Game changes how it handles information, for example by adding online features, we will update this policy and the dates above before that version is released.
